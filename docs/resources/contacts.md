# Contacts

This page is the single source for every LUMC link mentioned in the guide, so it doubles as an overview of where to go for what:
who to ask, what to use, and which rules apply.

## Who to contact

| Contact | Token | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| [LUMC Research Support Desk (RSD)](https://www.albinusnet.nl/en/products-and-services/research/research-facilities/research-support-desk/) ([contact form](https://topdesk.lumc.nl/tas/public/ssp/content/serviceflow?unid=192bd5baf12d4055bf50f355cebfa768)) | `{{rsd}}` | Central place for research support; routes questions to the relevant experts. Usually the fastest route for routine privacy and data questions. |
| [LUMC Data Stewardship](https://www.albinusnet.nl/actueel/themas/datastewardship/) | `{{data-stewardship}}` | General information on data stewardship. |
| [LUMC Research Software Stewardship](https://www.albinusnet.nl/en/products-and-services/research/data-stewardship/research-software-stewardship/) | `{{rs-stewardship}}` | General information on research software stewardship. |
| [LUMC DCC](https://www.albinusnet.nl/actueel/themas/lumc-dcc-data-competence-center/) ([LUMCDCC@lumc.nl](mailto:LUMCDCC@lumc.nl)) | `{{dcc}}` | The LUMC Data Competence Center, and questions about this guide. |
| [LUMC Privacy team](https://www.albinusnet.nl/weten-en-regelen/juridische-zaken/privacy/privacy-algemeen/) ([privacy@lumc.nl](mailto:privacy@lumc.nl)) | `{{privacy}}` | Privacy and GDPR questions, and finding your privacy officer. Expect long waits; route routine questions through the RSD instead. |
| LUMC data-breach reporting procedure ([info page](https://www.albinusnet.nl/en/products-and-services/juridische-zaken/privacy/reporting-a-data-breach/), [QMS document](https://lumc.zenya.work/QC/65-NR-78)) | `{{data-breach}}` | Reporting a suspected data breach. |
| [Techno Infopoint MDR/IVDR (TIP)](https://www.albinusnet.nl/weten-en-regelen/medische-technologie/Gebruikvanapparatuur/MedischeTechnologie-MDR-IVDR-ICT) ([techno-infopoint@lumc.nl](mailto:techno-infopoint@lumc.nl)) | `{{regulatory}}` | Software that may be a medical device: SaMD, MDR, IVDR, FDA, and comparable regimes. |
| [LURIS Legal](https://www.albinusnet.nl/organisatie/directoraten/onderzoek-valorisatie/juridisch-advies) ([lurislegal@lumc.nl](mailto:lurislegal@lumc.nl)) | `{{technology-transfer}}` | Patents, intellectual property, and commercialization. Contact before any public disclosure. |
| [ITenDI_Security@lumc.nl](mailto:ITenDI_Security@lumc.nl) | `{{security}}` | Security questions, including help with a SIA. |
| [IT&DI service desk](https://topdesk.lumc.nl/tas/public/ssp/content/serviceflow?unid=10fb0daafec54f83a24996588c11bc63&from=b6bb483f-b141-43a9-809d-6cf51b93fea9) | `{{itdi}}` | IT support, infrastructure, and deployment help. Describe the question thoroughly; triage is good. |
